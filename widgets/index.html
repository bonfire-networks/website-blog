Widgets

Bring Bonfire stories and federated conversations to your website. Open source, and yours to make your own.

  • The Telling

    Add federated comments to anything you publish. Readers reply with their existing fediverse account, and the conversation lives on your Bonfire server.

    Get the embed code

    Replace your-instance.social with your Bonfire address:

    <script
  src="https://your-instance.social/js/comments_embed.js"
  data-theme="dark"
  data-mode="nested"
  async
></script>
    Explore The Telling →

  • Spotlight

    Give your community’s stories a place to shine. Pin posts on Bonfire and display them on your website in a browsable carousel.

    Get the embed code

    Replace your-instance.social with your Bonfire address:

    <script
  src="https://your-instance.social/js/pins_embed.js"
  data-variant="carousel"
  data-theme="light"
  async
></script>

    Choose light or dark to suit your website.
