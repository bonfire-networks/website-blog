Open science infrastructure, shaped by the communities who rely on it.

We partner with universities, scholarly networks and science communicators who run spaces of their own, co-design around real practice, and research how communities actually work, such as experimenting with new forms of governance and open knowledge exchange.

Our collaborations span living labs at Warwick, governance research with Princeton HCI and design experiments with the Prosocial Design Network. The code, design patterns and methods we develop return to the commons, openly licensed.

The Open Science Network

Bonfire, assembled for scholarship.

The Open Science Network is a ready-made set of building blocks, put together for research and governed by the researchers who use it, shaped with the scholarly organisations above. Université de Lorraine and the Paris region digital university network (UNIF) are among the institutions lining up next. A university, a scholarly society, a lab or a collective can each run its own instance and stay federated with all the others.

Professional identity

Sign in with ORCID, Zenodo or your institution's own login. Publications, affiliations and research interests arrive from ORCID and OpenAlex rather than being retyped.

Conversations worth keeping

Posts and discussions can take DOIs and become citable FAIR objects, preserved as part of the scholarly record instead of lost in a private thread.

Data sovereignty

Each community runs its own instance. Your data stays under your control, and you connect globally without giving up local governance.

Connected knowledge

Share a paper and watch it come alive: metadata, co-authors, datasets and related works. Colleagues join in from whatever platform they already use.

Extensible

Modular by design. Add repository connectors, peer review workflows or research tools as your community finds it needs them.

Co-design & research

Something to build on, and to study.

“We're not just piloting a platform, we're piloting the process.”

Warwick Methods Fellowship · A Living Lab for the Federated Research Commons

Co-design with us

Everything we build is free software under the AGPL, so anything designed for you lands in the commons for everyone. The shape is whatever suits: a grant, a consortium, a fellowship, a living lab like Warwick's, or Mosaic, our co-design programme. Either way the output is public infrastructure, rather than a deliverable that rots when the project ends.

Software you can shape

Because it's modular the whole way down, an intervention can ship as an extension and run on a live instance without forking the entire app. Trying something new takes hours rather than months, and what works becomes an extension anyone can install.

Governance you can observe

Custom roles, per-group rules, and decisions from simple polls to structured consensus. Communities governing themselves in ways no commercial platform will let you watch, let alone alter.
