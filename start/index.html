Set up Bonfire: three ways in.

Everything you need to set up and configure a new Bonfire instance. Pick the route that matches how much you want to run yourself.

You run it

Install Bonfire on your own

For those who want to self-host their own Bonfire instance. You'll need to be comfortable with the command line.

  • Full control, on your own infrastructure
  • AGPL, free forever
  • Docker-based deployment
◼ good if: you have a server & some sysadmin comfort

New to servers? We run guided install parties & 1-on-1 setup.

Cooperative hosting

Join a nascent co-operative ecosystem to operate a sustainable, resilient infrastructure for Bonfire development, hosting, and support.

  • No servers to look after
  • Updates & backups handled
  • Co-op infrastructure, not a corporate cloud
◼ good if: you want your space, not a sysadmin job

We build it with you

Go further with Mosaic

Rely on the Bonfire team to build a tailored digital space for your community and support you through the whole process.

  • Co-design workshops with your people
  • Your identity, governance & integrations
  • Training, ongoing care & co-op hosting
◼ good if: your community needs a space shaped around it
