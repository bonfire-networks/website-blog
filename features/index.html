Bonfire/Features

Bonfire's building blocks, at a glance.

There's no single Bonfire app with a fixed feature list. It's a kit of composable blocks: switch on what your community needs, leave out the rest. Here's a tour of some the essential features in the kit, including some of what we haven't finished yet. Weighing it against alternative tools for communities? See how Bonfire compares →

Post & converse Feeds & discovery Circles & boundaries Safety & moderation Make it yours Federation & reach Your data Beyond the microblog

Post & converse

Room to say it properly.

A composer that fits each kind of post, and threads that let a conversation branch out and stay legible as it grows.

A powerful composer

Short updates or long-form posts. Add a title to start a thread, mention people, format your text, and share a quick thought or a full blog post.

Markdown friendly

Rich formatting, so you can emphasise a point and give your writing a structure.

Any media

Enrich posts with photos, video, audio, and other files.

Branching threads

Replies can branch into sub-threads, so you follow a discussion without losing the context it grew from.

Beta

Edit your posts

Made a typo, or want to add context? Edit after publishing instead of deleting and reposting.

Beta

Collaborate on posts

Some posts are better written together. Write with your peers, or simply invite a trusted friend to check your spelling.

Feeds & discovery

The feed is yours to build.

No mysterious algorithm ranking your people. You assemble the feeds you want, out of the filters that matter to you, and keep the ones that work.

Custom feeds

Build ad-hoc feeds with a simple interface: filter and sort by content type, circle, date, engagement, source instance, and more.

Saved presets

Keep the feeds you built, name them, and arrange them however you like.

Full-text search

Find people, posts, and everything else that has been indexed on your instance.

Hashtags & mentions

Tag what you post and reach the people you mean, across the whole network.

Topics

Curated categories to organise conversation, so a subject has a home instead of scrolling past.

Follow anyone

Follow people on any federated platform, at your own pace. Your network isn't limited to who else signed up here.

Circles & boundaries

Who sees what is your call.

Most networks give you one blunt switch: public, or not. Bonfire helps you foster your relationships, and lets every post carry its own rules.

How boundaries work, in the docs →

Circles

Lists of people: "local friends", "the team", "mutual aid crew". Each circle has its own feed, and represents the many-sided relationships you really have.

Boundaries

Choose who can see, reply, quote, or even edit a post. Make it public but accept replies only from one circle, for instance.

Custom roles

Create roles and grant them to people or circles: what they can view, how they can interact, and anything else they may do.

Interoperable controls

Boundaries speak the same language as GoToSocial interaction controls and Mastodon quote authorisations, so they hold up off your instance too.

Direct messages

Private conversations, one to one or with a group.

Multiple profiles

Several separate profiles on one account: different personas, or your org and your project alongside you.

Shared profiles

Give an organisation or project its own profile, and share access with the rest of your team.

Safety & moderation

Hold the line, together.

Moderation burns people out when it lands on one pair of shoulders. These are tools for spreading the weight, and for refusing the designs that make a place hostile in the first place.

Read about the federated moderation toolkit project →

Flag & review

Anyone can flag; moderators see a queue and can remove what doesn't belong.

Silence, ghost, or block

Turn someone down, make yourself invisible to them, or cut them off entirely, at the user level or the whole instance.

Shared blocklists

Lean on the work other communities have already done, rather than each of you meeting the same bad actor alone.

No dark patterns

Switch off follower counts, reaction counts, and infinite scroll. The things engineered to keep you hooked are optional here.

Govern, your way

Custom roles, shared profiles, and consent-based tools, so power is distributed the way your community actually decides things.

Defaults, not dictates

Admins set the defaults; every member can see exactly how the instance is configured, and override it for themselves.

Make it yours

Assembled from blocks, by you.

Bonfire is made of pluggable extensions all the way down. Even the core. Add what you need, fork what almost fits, drop what you don't want. No two communities end up with the same space.

Browse every extension →

Extensions

Every piece of Bonfire is a composable building block. Users enable and disable them; admins install new ones, fork them, or write their own.

Browse the extensions →

Themes

Change the look and feel: pick a theme, or make your own. No coding required.

Granular settings

Tune how the app behaves in detail, overriding the choices made by designers, developers, and even your instance admins.

Flavours

Start from a preset that's already close to what you need (social, community, coordination, open science), then adjust block by block.

See the flavours →

Federated comments

Embed the Telling on any static site: readers reply from Bonfire, Mastodon, or any ActivityPub account.

How the Telling works →

Single sign-on

Speaks OpenID Connect and OAuth2 as both a client and a provider: members sign in with an account they already have, and your Bonfire can sign them in to your other apps.

Build your own

Documented libraries for accounts, boundaries, social, ActivityPub and economic flows. If it doesn't exist yet, you can write it.

Federation & reach

Open to the world, as far as you like.

Your space is yours to open wide or keep closed. You decide who's invited through the door.

Local & invite-only

Quiet and warm, a space just for your people. Nobody joins your bonfire unless you say so.

Beta

Archipelago mode

Federate only with allies and communities you trust, and form a private interconnected network together.

About archipelago mode →

Fully federated

ActivityPub, so you can follow and be followed across Mastodon, PeerTube, Mobilizon, and every other Bonfire.

Bridges beyond

Protocol bridges reach Bluesky and the wider ATmosphere, so nobody is left out.

Bridged via BridgyFed →

Your data

Nothing here is holding you hostage.

A space can't be turned against its people when everyone is free to leave with everything. Moving instances shouldn't mean starting from scratch, so it doesn't.

Import everything

Bring your account, your follows, and even your past posts from another instance, whether it runs Bonfire, Mastodon, or another fediverse app. Most tools can't move your posts across; this one can.

Export everything

Posts, relationships, settings, all of it. Your data belongs to you and you are not locked into any app or instance.

Identify migration

Move your profile to a new home and stay yourself: your followers are redirected, your old address points to the new one, and your identity carries across intact.

AGPL, super-forkable

No VC, no ads, nothing to sell. If we ever go bad, the code is already yours to take.

Beyond the microblog

Blocks for what your community does.

Talking is only the start. These blocks create a collaborative place where things get organised, decided, made, and kept. Several are still being built. We'd rather say so than pretend.

Community rules

Shared expectations for an instance or a group, so newcomers know the space they're entering and moderators have something concrete to point to.

Announcements

Broadcast to every member: a call to action, an urgent update, or a downtime warning lands in notifications and as a push, rather than hoping it surfaces in the feed.
