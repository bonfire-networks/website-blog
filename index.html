Every space takes shape in three acts.

  1. 01 Gather Claim your space A home that is truly yours to govern.
  2. 02 Shape it Pick your building blocks Fit the tools to how your community works.
  3. 03 Connect Open the doors Connect to specific communities or the entire open social web.

Gather

First, a place that's truly yours.

The mythical "global town square" is fine for chatter, but lousy for organising. When you need specific people to see something, act on it, and follow through, you need a place of your own: with members, shared rules, a calendar, and a way to decide things together.

From day one, you set the rules, who joins, who sees what, who decides, what features you need, how you configure them. No mysterious algorithm, no landlord who can sell the building from under you. And if you ever want to move, you take everything with you.

Decide together

Govern, your way

Custom roles, shared profiles, and consent-based tools. Distribute power to match how your community makes decisions.

Safety without burnout

Hold the line, together

Circles and boundaries decide who can see and reply; shared blocklists and moderation tools spread the load, so no one carries it alone.

Built in anti-enshittification

Take everything with you

Import and export all your data: posts, relationships, settings. A space can't be turned against its people when everyone is free to leave with everything.

Shining a light on the power of defaults

Defaults, not dictates

Admins define the defaults; every member can see exactly how the space is configured, and override settings for themselves.

Shape it

Then, made to fit, by you.

Bonfire is assembled from the building blocks you pick. Include what your community needs, leave out the rest, give it your look and feel, and it becomes a space that's unmistakably yours. No two communities end up the same.

Your domain, your name, your look. End to end. Members arrive at your space, not a precarious corner of a platform you don't control.

No two end up alike, see how different communities made it their own →

Connect it

Then, open to the world, as far as you choose.

Many interconnected worlds, not one massive platform. From day one your space can be linked to millions of people across the open social web, so your community doesn't start from scratch. No central power dictates the rules. Autonomous spaces, connected on their terms.

You decide exactly how open or closed to be:

01

Your local community

Invite-only, quiet and warm, a space just for your people. Nobody else joins your bonfire unless you say so.

02

A trusted archipelago

Connected only with your friends, allies and communities you trust, and form a private interconnected network together (see archipelago mode).

03

The open web

Follow and be followed across every other Bonfire, Mastodon, PeerTube, etc, (the fediverse), with bridges to ATproto/Bluesky and more, so no one is left out.
