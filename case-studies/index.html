Jacobin: From audience to community

What they were after

Readers who debate, organise and build solidarity together, not just an audience that reads an article and then leaves.

A comment section wasn't going to be enough, and neither was sending readers to a platform someone else owns. So they kept publishing articles on Ghost while giving their community a home for real conversations: federated, in their own hands, organised with the groups, topics, and boundaries their community actually needed.
